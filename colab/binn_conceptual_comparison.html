🚀 Enhanced BINN Advantages
🧬 Genetic Precision
  • Individual SNP-level analysis
  • Captures rare variants
  • Genetic predisposition mapping
  • Population stratification aware
🏥 Clinical Integration
  • Demographics (age, sex)
  • Lifestyle factors (BMI, smoking)
  • Comorbidities (diabetes, hypertension)
  • Family history integration
🔍 Enhanced Interpretability
  • SNP-level feature importance
  • Clinical factor contributions
  • Genetic vs environmental effects
  • Pathway-specific insights
🎯 Comprehensive Modeling
  • Multi-modal risk assessment
  • Personalized predictions
  • Biological constraint preservation
  • Scalable to diverse populations

🎯 Core Innovation

Enhanced BINN extends traditional protein-pathway BINN by integrating SNP-level genetic variants with clinical phenotypes, enabling comprehensive risk modeling while preserving biological interpretability through individual layer architecture.
